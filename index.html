Thank you for using Sajda: Daily Athan & Qibla ("Daily Athan", "we", "us", or "our"). Daily Athan is an Islamic lifestyle application designed to provide prayer times, Qibla direction, Quran-related content, Islamic dates, prayer notifications, location-based prayer calculations, streak features, and other Islamic features.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it may be stored or shared, and the choices available to you when you use the Daily Athan application.

By using Daily Athan, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the application's features. Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Daily Athan may process the following categories of information.

1.1 Location Information

Daily Athan may request access to your device's location to provide location-based Islamic services, including:

  • Local prayer times
  • Sunrise and sunset times
  • Qibla direction
  • Local Islamic calendar information
  • Nearby city/town identification
  • Location-based prayer calculations

Depending on your device and permission settings, the application may use approximate location or precise location. Location access is requested only when necessary for location-dependent functionality.

You may choose to deny location permission. If you do so, location-dependent features may not work automatically. You can also manually select a city or location where supported by the application.

Background Location: Daily Athan may access your location in the background to schedule prayer time notifications even when the app is not actively in use. This allows the app to send you prayer reminders at the correct times for your location. You can disable background location access through your device settings at any time.

1.2 Manually Selected Location

If you manually search for and select a city, town, or country, the selected location may be stored locally on your device so that Daily Athan can continue calculating prayer times for your selected location. You can change your selected location at any time through the application.

1.3 Anonymous Device Identifier

Daily Athan may generate and store a randomly created anonymous device identifier on your device. This identifier is not linked to your personal identity and is used solely for:

  • Maintaining your streak data and app preferences
  • Providing consistent app functionality across sessions
  • Technical diagnostics and crash reporting

This anonymous identifier cannot be used to identify you personally and is stored only on your device using local storage.

1.4 Device and App Information

Depending on the services enabled in the application, limited technical information may be processed to maintain application functionality, security, reliability, and performance. This may include:

  • App version
  • Operating system version
  • Basic technical information
  • Error or diagnostic information

We do not use this information to identify you personally unless required for a specific feature or service and clearly disclosed.

1.5 User-Provided Information

If you choose to use streak or social features, you may provide:

  • Display name (optional, for streak features)
  • Prayer completion data (for streak tracking)

This information is stored on our servers and is used solely to provide streak functionality. You can request deletion of this data at any time (see Section 17).

1.6 Prayer and Religious Preferences

Daily Athan may store your prayer calculation preferences (such as calculation method and Asr school) and notification settings. These preferences may indicate your religious practice patterns. This data is stored locally on your device and is used solely to provide customized prayer time calculations and notifications.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Daily Athan may be used for the following purposes:

Providing Prayer Times

Location information may be used to calculate prayer times according to your selected location and prayer calculation settings. Depending on your device and settings, prayer times may be calculated via our backend API or directly via the AlAdhan API when device coordinates are available.

Providing Qibla Direction

Location information may be used to determine the appropriate Qibla direction for your location.

Providing Islamic Dates

Your selected location and device settings may be used to provide relevant Islamic and Gregorian date information.

Location Search and Map

If you use the location search or map picker feature, search queries and map tile requests may be sent to OpenStreetMap and its partners to provide location search results and map imagery.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Daily Athan may send notifications such as prayer reminders, prayer time notifications, Islamic reminders, and other notifications that you explicitly enable. You can disable notifications through the application or your device's notification settings.

Streak Features

If you choose to use streak features, your prayer completion data and display name may be stored on our servers to track your streak progress and enable social streak features with friends and family.

Quran Content

Daily Athan provides Quran content through a combination of local data bundles and backend API services:

  • Quran Text: Quran Arabic text (Uthmani script) is provided locally through the quran package, sourced from the Tanzil Project under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
  • Quran Translations and Tafsir: These are fetched through the app's backend API, which uses licensed third-party content providers.
  • Quran Audio: Quran audio/recitation URLs are fetched through the app's backend API. Audio streaming is provided by third-party audio hosting services. We do not host Quran audio files on our own servers.

3. Notifications

Daily Athan may request notification permission to provide prayer reminders and other notifications. Notifications may include Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha reminders, sunrise information, and other Islamic reminders. You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Some application preferences may be stored locally on your device. These may include selected location, prayer calculation settings, language preference, theme preference, notification preferences, prayer reminder settings, and other application settings. Locally stored preferences generally remain on your device unless you clear the application's data or uninstall the application.

Daily Athan provides Islamic content from multiple sources:

Quran Text

Quran Arabic text (Uthmani script) is provided through the quran package, sourced from the Tanzil Project under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. We do not claim ownership of this content. https://tanzil.net

Quran Translations and Tafsir

Quran translations and Tafsir are fetched through the app's backend API from licensed third-party content providers. We do not claim ownership of this content.

Other Islamic Content

Daily Athan also provides hadith, duas, wazifa, and Islamic events through the app's backend API. This content is managed and curated by the app's content providers.

Content Attribution

We do not claim ownership of third-party content where ownership belongs to its respective copyright holder. If you believe any content violates copyright, please contact us for removal.

6. Third-Party Services

Daily Athan may use third-party services, APIs, libraries, or infrastructure to provide certain application functionality. These services may process limited technical or functional information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Third-party services may include:

  • Local Quran Data Bundle (quran package) — Provides Quran Arabic text locally. Sourced from Tanzil Project under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
  • Backend Quran Content API — Provides Quran translations, Tafsir, and audio recitations through the app's own backend. This backend uses licensed third-party content providers.

15. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe Daily Athan has processed personal information associated with you and you would like to request deletion or clarification regarding that information, contact us at: support@sajdadailyathan.site.

Please include enough information for us to understand your request. Where applicable, we will review and respond to your request in accordance with applicable laws and the technical capabilities of our services.

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • In-App Deletion: Use the "Delete My Data" option in the app's Settings → Privacy & Data section. This will delete both your local data and your account data stored on our servers, including your streak data, prayer completion history, and display name.
  • Clearing Local Data: Clearing the application's data through Android settings will remove all locally stored preferences and data.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Daily Athan will remove all locally stored data from your device. Server-side data associated with your account will remain until you request deletion via email.

When you use the in-app "Delete My Data" feature, we will permanently delete the following server-side data:

  • Your user account and display name
  • All streak data and prayer completion history
  • All shared streak data and member associations

16. Consent and Permissions

Daily Athan requests permissions only when they are needed for specific features:

  • Location Permission (Fine + Coarse): Used for automatic location-based prayer times and Qibla direction functionality.
  • Notification Permission: Used for prayer reminders and Islamic notifications.
  • Exact Alarm Permission: Used for precise prayer time notifications.
  • Boot Completed Permission: Used to reschedule notifications after device restart.
  • Vibrate Permission: Used for Tasbih vibration feedback.
  • Wake Lock Permission: Used to keep screen active during specific features.

You can refuse or revoke permissions through Android settings, although doing so may limit certain features.
