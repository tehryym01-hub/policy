🔒 Data Deletion Policy

Data That Will Be Erased

Upon successful processing of your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • All personal identifiers linked to your account

Certain transactional or financial logs may be retained temporarily for legal and regulatory compliance.

Processing Timeframe

Requests are processed within 7 to 14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been fully deleted.
